# Brand document: Brambleway Tutoring

Version: 1

**In short.** Brambleway gives children a tutor.

## 1. Purpose

Patient tutoring that leverages local talent.

- **Why it matters.** Children deserve attention.

## Who it is for

Parents who want a better tutor, described before the answer is given.

| Circle | Who |
|---|---|
| 4 | parents |

## Category

**A tutoring service.**

- **What that means.** Tutoring.

## Positioning statement

**For parents, Brambleway is a tutoring service. It keeps one tutor per child.**

- **For** parents.
- **Who** want simpler tutoring.
- **That** keeps one tutor.
- **Unlike** agencies.

## Personality

**Five traits.**

1. **Patient.** We are patient, but not slow.
2. **Human, but not soft.** Kind.
3. **Frank.** We're frank, but not cruel, and parents thank us for it.
4. **Local but not parochial.** Our tutors live nearby and know the schools.
5. **Steady.** Steady, but not rigid: the same tutor every week, and a swap when a real reason comes up.
6. **Hopeful.** Not: hopeful, but not naive; we expect the child to turn a corner and we say so.

## Voice

**Plain.**

- **Patient.** Not: "journeys." We write: "pace."
- **Frank.** Not: "portal." We write: "two sentences."
- **Local.** We write: "nearby."
