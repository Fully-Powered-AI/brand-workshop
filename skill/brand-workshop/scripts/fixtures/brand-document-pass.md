# Brand strategy: Brambleway Tutoring

What Brambleway Tutoring stands for, who it is for, and how it sounds; every line chosen by its owner, none of it tested with buyers yet. An invented company, used only to exercise the check script.

**In short.** We exist to give every child who is behind at school a tutor who knows them by name. Families have more tutoring options than ever and less idea which one will stick, because most sell hours, not a person. We are a local tutoring service that pairs each child with one tutor for a whole school year. The same tutor every week, chosen for the child and kept for the year, is what makes that possible.

## Purpose

**We exist to give every child who is behind at school a tutor who knows them by name.**

- **Why it matters.** At our best, a child who dreaded Thursday maths asks for an extra session, because the tutor noticed what was actually in the way.
- **The tension we hold.** Families want results they can measure and a person their child trusts; the market sells the first by the hour and treats the second as luck. We hold both by keeping the pairing for the year.
- **What it settles.** Whether to offer drop-in sessions with whichever tutor is free: no.

## Who it is for

**Parents of a child aged seven to fourteen whose last school report flagged one subject, who have already tried an app or a worksheet pack and stopped.**

- **The buyer.** A parent who books the first session after a parents' evening, pays monthly, and judges by whether the child stops resisting.
- **The wider market.** Every family with a school-age child; families who pay for anything outside school; families who have paid for tutoring before.
- **Who else is in the room.** The child, who decides in the second week whether this will stick; sometimes a grandparent who pays.
- **The best sign of fit.** A parent who has kept a notebook of what the teacher said at each parents' evening.
- **A picture of them.** Priya, a nurse in her forties, has a ten-year-old whose report said "losing confidence in maths". She tried a subscription app for two months; it is still installed. She wants one person she can message after a bad week. Priya is invented.

## Category

**A local tutoring service: one tutor per child, kept for the school year.**

- **What that means.** We sell the pairing, not the hour; a family buys a year with one person, and the sessions follow.
- **What we sell against.** Tutoring agencies that send whoever is free, and the apps families try first and abandon.
- **Why this category.** An existing category the owner can be best in locally, because no agency in the area keeps one tutor on one child for a year; a new category would cost years of explaining.
- **What we declined.** "Learning coaching" (nobody searches for it); "the anti-app" (defined by what it is not).

## Positioning statement

**For parents of a child aged seven to fourteen who have tried an app or a worksheet pack and watched it stall, Brambleway Tutoring is a local tutoring service that pairs the child with one tutor for the whole school year, unlike agencies that send whoever is free, because the tutor is chosen for the child and kept.**

Its parts:

- **For** parents of a child aged seven to fourteen whose last school report flagged one subject.
- **Who** have tried an app or a worksheet pack and watched it stall, and now want a person.
- **Brambleway Tutoring is** a local tutoring service.
- **That** pairs the child with one tutor for the whole school year, so the child stops resisting.
- **Unlike** agencies that send whoever is free, or the app still installed on the tablet.
- **Because of** the pairing: the tutor is chosen for the child in a first session the family does not pay for, and kept for the year.

## Personality

**Five traits, each with its edge.**

1. **Patient.** We go at the child's pace and say so to the parent, and we set a date to look at progress together, so patience never becomes drift.
2. **Frank.** We tell a parent what we saw in the session, including the bad weeks, in two sentences they can act on; frankness that wounds a child is not ours.
3. **Local.** Our tutors live within a bus ride of the families they teach and know the schools by name; we do not pretend to know a school we have never visited.
4. **Steady.** The same tutor, the same evening, every week; steady is not rigid, and a swap for a real reason is arranged in a day.
5. **Hopeful.** We expect the child to turn a corner and we say so; hope that promises a grade is a promise we do not make.

## Voice

**Short sentences a parent could repeat at the school gate.** Each trait, with what we avoid and what we write instead:

- **Patient.** Not: "Our personalised learning journeys adapt to every learner's pace." We write: "We go at your child's pace, and we tell you where that pace is."
- **Frank.** Not: "Session feedback is delivered via our parent engagement portal." We write: "After each session you get two sentences from the tutor about what happened."
- **Local.** Not: "A nationwide network of vetted education professionals." We write: "Your tutor lives nearby and knows your child's school."
- **Steady.** Not: "Flexible scheduling with our on-demand tutor pool." We write: "Same tutor, same evening, every week."
- **Hopeful.** Not: "Guaranteed grade improvement in twelve weeks!" We write: "Most children turn a corner. We will tell you when yours does."
