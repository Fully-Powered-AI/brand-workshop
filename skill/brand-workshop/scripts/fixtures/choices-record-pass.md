## Decision: adjectives

Chosen: Local. Our tutors live within a bus ride of the families they teach and know the schools by name; we do not pretend to know a school we have never visited.
Chosen: Steady. The same tutor, the same evening, every week; steady is not rigid, and a swap for a real reason is arranged in a day.
Chosen: Hopeful. We expect the child to turn a corner and we say so; hope that promises a grade is a promise we do not make.
