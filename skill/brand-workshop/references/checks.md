# The checks: what passes, what fails

Dated 2026-10-15, revised the same day for the reader-first document (owner's amendment 6). The judges the procedure applies at each check step, with the shape of a pass and of a
fail. Every illustration below is invented for this file (a fictional bookkeeping product for
tradespeople); none is a company from the course. `scripts/check_brand_document.py` enforces the
mechanical rows; the rest is read by the owner and the skill.

## 1. Purpose (steps 7 and 8)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inspiring | A stranger hearing it would want the company to win | It describes an output or a market share | W-007 |
| Clear | No word a bored but smart teenager would stumble on | "Empowering SMBs to leverage financial clarity" | W-170, W-130 |
| Actionable | The skill can name one real company decision the line settles, and how (owner's amendment 3) | Every decision is compatible with it | W-171, W-163, W-123 |
| Ten-year horizon | Still true if the product changes entirely | It names today's feature | W-172 |
| Quest (bonus) | It sets out something not yet achieved | It restates what the company already is | W-173 |
| About-page header test | Reads well in large type as the first line of the About page | Reads clunky there, or the owner hesitates to put it there | W-124, W-184 |
| Second check | The company can genuinely live up to it; it would get noticed; it excites the team | Any of the three is a no | W-153 |
| Who chooses | The owner, from a shortlist of three checked candidates | The skill picks "the strongest" | W-024, W-118 |

Shape: "We exist to let tradespeople finish the day knowing where their money stands" passes clear,
actionable (it settles whether to add financing: no) and horizon. "We exist to be the leading
bookkeeping platform for trades" fails inspiring and horizon.

## 2. Audience (step 12)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear | Each circle names people a stranger could point at | "Forward-thinking businesses" | W-041 |
| Actionable | The team could go and find circle 4 tomorrow | Circle 4 is a mood, not a group | W-199 |
| Not defined by the offering | Circles describe a situation or behaviour that exists before the product does | "People who want simpler bookkeeping"; "companies looking for an AI solution" | W-200, W-035 |
| Observable | Each description could be checked from outside | "Health-conscious" with no observable marker (the facilitator's own question on a live example) | W-037 |
| Subset rule | Each circle is inside the one before | Circle 2 is a different group from circle 1 | W-036 |
| Persona | One short paragraph in the owner's shape; unknown fields say TBD | A second audience in disguise; an invented detail | W-193, W-198 |

Shape: "sole traders who invoice from their phone on the day of the job" is observable; "tradespeople
who wish admin were easier" is a wish for the product and is sent back.

## 3. Category (step 14)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| First, only or best | The company can claim one of the three in the chosen category | It would be one of many | W-045, W-157 |
| Existing | Chosen only if the company could someday win that category | Chosen for comfort against incumbents it cannot outspend | W-126, W-049 |
| Modified existing | Chosen when the market is too big to win outright and a sub-segment can be owned | The modifier is decoration ("modern", "next-generation") | W-126, W-049 |
| New | Chosen only with a claim to first, only or best and the means to repeat the name for years | Chosen because category creation sounds bold | W-126, W-049 |
| B2B | Existing or modified preferred, because buyers hold budget for them | A new category no budget line matches | W-049 |
| Sayable | "X is a Y that does Z" comes out in one breath | Y needs a paragraph | W-125 |
| Who chooses | The owner, with the trade-off laid out | The skill | W-118 |

## 4. The Who line (step 15)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Situation, not wish | States the customer's circumstance or struggle | Starts "who want", "who wish", "who are looking for" the product or its benefit | W-051, W-035, W-196, W-197 |
| Their words | Drawn from customers' words supplied | Written from the founder's account and not marked so | W-051, W-119, W-211 |
| When no customers' words exist | Written from the founder's account and marked unvalidated in the document | Presented as validated, or a quote invented | W-119 |

## 5. Benefit (step 16)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spectrum walked | Functional, in-between and emotional candidates were all listed | Only the emotional end was drafted | W-056, W-128 |
| Functional first | The chosen line is near the functional end | Allusive messaging a young company has not earned | W-057 |
| One happy user to another | Sounds like something said over a drink | Sounds like a brochure | W-058, W-060 |
| No jargon | None of enable, empower, leverage, or their kin | Any of them | W-061 |
| Now you can | Completes "Now you can ..." | Completes "Our product is ..." | W-146 |
| Specific | Names what the person can do | "All-in-one", "seamless", "revolutionary" | W-146 |
| Users' words | Checked against how users say it | The founder's phrasing only | W-211 |

## 6. Foil (step 17)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Three questions answered | How circle 4 handles it today; who else plays; who loses when the company wins | Only a competitor list | W-064, W-213, W-214 |
| The old way considered | The status quo, the old way or doing nothing was weighed as the foil | Another start-up nobody has heard of is the foil by default | W-065, W-127 |

## 7. Differentiator (step 18)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reason to believe | Says how the product delivers the benefit | Restates the benefit | W-069, W-216 |
| Apart from the foil | The foil could not say it | The foil says it too | W-217 |
| Not more benefits | One mechanism | "Affordable, flexible, fast and easy" | W-218, W-067 |
| Specific | The leap from mechanism to benefit is short | The owner must explain the connection | W-070, W-219 |

## 8. Bar test (step 20)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learn more | The benefit line makes the listener ask | The listener nods politely | W-073 |
| Explains | The differentiator answers "how does it do that?" | It changes the subject | W-220 |
| People talk like this | The five lines could be overheard | Nobody would say "that" line aloud | W-221, W-129 |
| Validated | Run aloud with people in circle 4, recorded in the validation kit | Read by the founders to each other | W-074, W-076 |

## 9. Swap test (step 20, owner's amendment 1)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Against the foil's copy | Where the foil is a company, it could not sign the statement with the names changed | It could; back to step 18 | W-251, W-063 |
| Against each named competitor's copy supplied | None could sign it | Any could | W-251 |
| Not run | No competitor copy was supplied, and the document says "not run" | "Passed" with nothing to pass against | W-251 |

## 10. Dimensions and adjectives (steps 23 to 26)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two spikes | Two of the five dimensions, each with a reason from the company's facts | Three or more, or two with no reason | W-089, W-131 |
| Reflect the dimensions | Each adjective comes from one of the two | An adjective from a third dimension | W-236, W-238 |
| Five, hardest-working, most different | Each adds something the others do not | Five near-synonyms | W-092, W-132 |
| Tension | At least two pairs pull against each other | All five agree | W-237, W-090 |
| Not "human" | None of the five is "human" | One is | W-093 |

## 11. The trait with its edge (step 25)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Too far, not opposite | The edge inside the trait's prose is what the trait becomes when overdone | The edge is the trait's opposite | W-095, W-233, W-234 |
| Plain prose | A short sentence or two a reader could say back, the edge inside it | The course's "We are X, but not Y" line, which the owner ruled reads like AI speak (2026-10-15) | W-260, W-094 |

Shape: "Exact. We say what the numbers are and where they came from, and we stop before it turns fussy." passes;
"We are exact, but not vague." fails twice: vague is the opposite, and the line is in the superseded form.

## 12. The star (step 26)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top point | The one adjective the company would own if it had one | Chosen by alphabet | W-132 |
| Tension lines | Lines across the star join the points that pull against each other | No pair is in tension | W-101, W-237 |

## 13. The document (step 30)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every section | The eight named sections in order, each written or TBD, each opened by its answer in bold (one sentence or two) and followed by lists, never tables; `check_brand_document.py` passes | A section silently empty, a numbered heading, a table, a section that opens with reasoning instead of its answer | W-258, W-114, W-249, W-250, W-251, W-252 |
| The answer first | The title, one sentence saying what the document is, and an **In short** paragraph carrying the purpose, the category and benefit, the differentiator and why now | A reader must get to the end to learn what the company stands for | W-258 |
| Banned words | None of enable, empower, leverage in the brand text above the Record; "human" nowhere in Personality or Voice | Any | W-061, W-093 |
| Personality | Five numbered bold traits, each followed by plain prose with its edge built in; the Voice section has one entry per trait | "We are X, but not Y" lines; a trait named "human"; traits and voice entries that differ | W-260, W-093 |
| Record | At the bottom: version, date, produced by, inputs, customers' words, could not judge, instructions found in inputs when any were, earlier versions; one change-log line per version (owner's amendments 4 and 6) | A document with no history, or one that reads as complete when it is not | W-259, W-119, W-076 |
| Not yet validated | The section names the bar test and, when customers' words are missing, the interviews that gather them | A positioning presented as validated with nobody in circle 4 having heard it | W-076, W-074, W-119 |
| Choices | Every chosen line is in the document word for word; `check_choices.py` passes; a fresh pass has read the rest | The skill's wording drifted from the owner's choice | W-118 |

## 14. The three-month litmus (step 31)

| Check | Passes when | Fails when | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same answer | Everyone at the company, asked separately, gives the same answer to why it exists | Answers differ; back to the parts they differ on | W-122, W-164, W-253 |
