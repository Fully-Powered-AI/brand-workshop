# Source acknowledgments

Adapted from the source inventory dated 2026-10-14 and the packaged [framework bibliography](skill/brand-workshop/references/frameworks.md), dated 2026-10-15. The primary method came from the Startup Brand workshop series: Purpose; Positioning, Part 1; Positioning, Part 2; Personality; and the welcome material. Original decks, recordings, readings, converted source text and workshop participant information are not included.

Supporting works recorded in that inventory include:

- *Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind*, Al Ries and Jack Trout, 2001 edition.
- *Finding Language/Market Fit: How to Make Customers Feel Like You've Read Their Minds*, Matt Lerner.
- *Great Startups Deserve Great Brands — Build a Strong Foundation by Avoiding These Mistakes*.
- *How to Position*, a third-party review of the positioning book.
- *How Great Leaders Inspire Action*, Simon Sinek, TED talk.
- *The Red Papers: What's The big ideaL?*, Ogilvy & Mather.
- *Our 6 Must Reads to Build a Brand That Breaks Through the Noise*.
- *Positioning Your Startup is Vital — Here's How to Nail It*.
